Theory

Spatial domain: g(x,y) = f(x,y) * h(x,y)

Fourier domain: G(u,v) = F(u,v)H(u,v)

Inverse transform: g(x,y) = F-1{F(u,v)H(u,v)}

Therefore, convolution in space is equivalent to multiplication in the Fourier domain.
